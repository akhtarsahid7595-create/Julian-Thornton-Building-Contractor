Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

Glasgow Drive Connect operates as a free driving instructor matching and connection service in Glasgow. We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when submitting an enquiry via WhatsApp or our online forms. This information may include your name, contact details, Glasgow postcode, lesson preference (manual/automatic), availability, and preferred test centre.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Match you with an available, DVSA-approved local driving instructor in your area.
  • Connect you directly with matched instructors so you can discuss lesson scheduling and pricing.
  • Respond to your customer support enquiries.

3. Data Sharing

We only share your submitted enquiry details with independent, DVSA-approved driving instructors operating in your specified Glasgow postcode area for the purpose of fulfilling your instructor matching request. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third-party marketers.

4. Instructor Relationship

Glasgow Drive Connect is a matching service. Independent driving instructors are self-employed or operate independent driving schools. Once connected, your driving tuition, payments, and lesson arrangements are governed directly between you and your instructor.
