Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Service Model Definition

Glasgow Drive Connect is a Driving Instructor Matching & Connection Service. We are not a traditional driving school and do not directly employ driving instructors or own driving school vehicles. Our service connects prospective learner drivers with independent, DVSA-approved driving instructors across Glasgow.

2. Free Matching Service

Instructor matching and connection through Glasgow Drive Connect is 100% free for learners. We do not charge learners any matching fee or hidden connection charges.

3. Lesson Pricing & Instructor Terms

Lesson prices, packages, payment terms, block booking discounts, and lesson schedules are set directly by individual driving instructors. Glasgow Drive Connect does not set or guarantee individual instructor prices.

4. Availability Disclaimer

All instructor matches, time slots, transmission preferences (manual/automatic), female instructor requests, and test centre coverage are subject to local instructor availability. We aim to connect you with an available instructor within 24 hours, but availability varies by postcode and demand.

5. Cancellations & Disputes

Once matched, any driving lesson cancellations, rescheduling, or refund requests must be handled directly between the learner driver and the matched instructor according to the instructor's individual business policies.
