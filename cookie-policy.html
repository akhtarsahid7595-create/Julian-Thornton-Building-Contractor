Cookie Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit our website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information to site owners.

2. How We Use Cookies

Glasgow Drive Connect operates as a driving instructor matching and connection service. We use cookies to:

  • Remember preferences you select (such as manual or automatic lessons) when using our lead matching form.
  • Analyze traffic and visitor interaction with our matching service using Google Analytics 4 (GA4) so we can optimize our marketing channels and page load speeds.

3. Analytics Cookies (GA4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to track user interaction, such as clicks on our WhatsApp buttons and form submissions. This data is collected anonymously and helps us measure the conversion rate of our matching service. The cookies set by GA4 include:

  • _ga: Used to distinguish users. Pervasive for 2 years.
  • _ga_*: Used to maintain session state. Pervasive for 1 year.

4. Controlling Cookies

You can manage and delete cookies through your web browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of our site, though you will still be able to submit inquiries directly via our primary WhatsApp link.
